# Updating the oliMoto website

Everything below is done in the browser. You never need to touch code.

## Signing in

1. Go to **olimoto.co.uk/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and sign in with the oliMoto GitHub account (or your own, if you've been added).
3. You'll see three sections on the left: **News**, **Supporters**, and **Dates and fundraising**.

When you click **Publish → Publish now**, the change goes live in about a minute. Every change is saved in the history, so nothing is ever lost; Christopher or Arch 35 can undo anything.

## Add a news post

1. Click **News**, then **News posts**.
2. Click **Add posts**. A new empty post appears at the top.
3. Fill in:
   - **Date**: the day it happened. If you only know the month, pick the 1st and tick **Show the month only**.
   - **Title**: a few words, like *Oli passes his A1 test*.
   - **Text**: what happened, in your own words.
   - **Photo** (optional): click **Choose an image** and upload one. Use photos of you both on the Hondas.
   - **Photo description**: one sentence saying what's in the photo, like *Oli and Joe with the new Hondas outside Shirlaws*. This is read out to people who can't see the screen.
4. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.

The newest eight posts with photos also show on the home page, under *The road to the start line*.

## Add a supporter

1. Click **Supporters**, then **Supporters**.
2. Click **Add supporters** and fill in the **Name**, **What they do** (a few words) and **Website** (starting with https://).
   - Write the **Name** exactly as the company writes it on its own website, capitals included (for example Lomo, IAM RoadSmart, OFFroad OFFcourse).
   - Add their **Logo**. The best place to get it is their own website. Then pick the **Logo tile colour**: white for a dark or coloured logo, dark for a white logo.
   - With no logo, the tile shows their name instead.
3. Drag it up or down the list to change the order. The order here is the order on the site.
4. Publish.

They appear on the Sponsor page and in the *Backed by* line at the bottom of the home page.

## The fundraising total

This updates by itself. The website reads the total from the JustGiving page about once an hour, so new donations show up without anyone doing anything.

The numbers under **Dates and fundraising** are only a backup, shown if JustGiving can't be reached. It's worth updating them every few months, or after a big milestone. If you ever move to a different JustGiving page, change the **JustGiving link** there and the total follows it.

## Change the departure date or time

Also in **Dates and fundraising**. Use the 24-hour clock for the time, in UK time (12:00 for noon). The countdown on every page, the map and the dates in the text all update together.

## Supporter announcement videos

Go to **olimoto.co.uk/studio**. Choose the supporter's logo, check the words, pick square (post) or story, and click **Download video**. It records for about 7 seconds and saves an MP4 you can post straight to Instagram or Facebook. The logo file stays on your computer.

## Supporter messages

Messages people send Oli and Joe wait for approval at **olimoto.co.uk/moderate**. Enter the moderation key, then **Approve** or **Reject** each one. Anything with swearing, insults or links is blocked before it gets there.

## If something looks wrong

- If a list in the editor shows **0** items, reload the page. It sometimes shows that while it's still loading.
- If the site hasn't changed two minutes after publishing, message Christopher or Arch 35.
