A father and son, riding the whole way together.

Oli rides for the record. Joe rides alongside him the whole way as support rider and camera crew, filming as they go.

Rider · Honda CB125R

Oli

"Oli has been riding mountain bikes and off-road motorbikes for years, with a single-minded dedication to improving his skills."

He takes on the ride between finishing school and starting university, and he's an Explorer Scout. He passed his CBT in November 2025 and spent the winter on the road with Joe behind him, talking him through it over the intercom.

Joe

"Who wouldn't want to travel the world, and what better way to do it than alongside your son?"
