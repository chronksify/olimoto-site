Privacy

Messages of support

When you send a message we store your name, the place you enter and your message. Joe reads it, and if he approves it, all three are shown publicly on the site. To keep spam down we also store a scrambled code made from your internet address for one hour. We don't store the address itself.

Sponsor and contact forms

The sponsor and contact forms send your details to the oliMoto team. We use them only to reply to you.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Removing your data

Email Joe and we'll delete your message or details.
