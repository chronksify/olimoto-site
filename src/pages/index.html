How it started

"It began with a simple bond, formed across muddy tracks, snowy mountain tops, and every adventure in between."

Joe has toured on motorbikes through 16 countries. Oli started out on a 50cc. The idea of riding around the world together came on the drive home from ABR Festival, and for months only a handful of people knew about it.

Then it got real. Oli passed his CBT in November 2025 and spent the winter on the road with Joe behind him, talking him through it over the intercom. In September 2026 he had his first lesson on a 125. In October he passed his A1 licence, and a week later two Hondas arrived: a CB125R for Oli and an NX500 for Joe.

When their story made Motorcycle News in September 2026, Joe wrote about his own dad.

"I grew up seeing my dad read it, copies in the work canteen and on the dashboard of the works van. … I am too late to share this moment with my dad, but being able to share it with my son makes it very special."

Joe Purkis

The way round

From Shirlaws to London, by boat to Portugal, then east across Europe and Asia to China. The bikes go on to Australia and New Zealand, then the USA from west to east, and home. About 19,000 miles through 20 countries.
