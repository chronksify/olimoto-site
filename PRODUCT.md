## Users

Confirmed with Chris, 17 November 2026.

- **Primary: a possible title sponsor.** A marketing or brand manager deciding whether this story is worth putting their name on. They arrive from an email, a press piece or a social post, usually on a laptop.
- **People who donate.** Members of the public who arrive from Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp, mostly on a phone, and might give to Save the Children.
- **Followers.** Friends, family, bikers and Scouts following the build-up now and the ride from July 2027.
- **Editors.** Joe Purkis updates news, supporters and dates himself through the editor at /admin. He is not technical.

## Product Purpose

oliMoto.co.uk is the home of Oli Purkis's attempt at the Guinness World Record for the youngest person to ride a motorcycle around the world. He rides with his dad, Joe, who is support rider and camera crew. They raise money for Save the Children.

Until the ride starts, the site covers the build-up. Success means a title sponsor gets in touch, donations go up, and people keep coming back for news.

## Positioning

A father and son doing this together. Oli is a teenager from Aberdeenshire who passed his CBT in November 2025 and his A1 licence in October 2026. Honda UK and Shirlaws back the ride. Their line: "For one, this is the adventure of a lifetime. For the other, it's the beginning of a lifetime of adventures."
